## Project: ReneWind  
### Course: Introduction to Neural Networks

### Project Overview
This project focuses on developing and improving classification models to predict **wind turbine generator failures** for ReneWind, a wind energy company. The objective is to enable **proactive maintenance strategies** that reduce operational costs and prevent unexpected turbine breakdowns.

Using sensor data collected from wind turbines, machine learning models are trained to identify patterns associated with potential generator failures. A key goal of the project is to **minimize false negatives**, ensuring that potential failures are detected early so that maintenance can be scheduled before a breakdown occurs.

The final models are evaluated and selected based on their ability to accurately predict failures and support **data-driven maintenance planning**, ultimately improving reliability and cost efficiency in wind energy production.

### Project Objective
The goal of this project is to develop neural network–based classification models that can:

- Predict wind turbine generator failures
- Reduce unexpected downtime
- Support proactive maintenance strategies
- Improve operational efficiency in wind energy systems
